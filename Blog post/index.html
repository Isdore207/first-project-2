Top tips on how to start a successful YouTube channel

YouTube is one of the most popular websites in the world, so it’s no surprise if you want to start a YouTube channel. You can find communities of viewers and creators in almost any niche imaginable — business, gaming, entertainment, sports, media. But before you can get one million subscribers, you need to know how to set up a YouTube channel for success. This guide has step-by-step instructions on creating a YouTube channel, tips on how to make your channel successful and examples of top YouTube channels you can draw inspiration from

How to start a YouTube channel

If you have a Google account, then you already have a YouTube account, which lets you watch and like videos and subscribe to channels. To publish your own videos, you must create a channel associated with your account. You can create either a personal or a business channel.

  1. Creating a personal YouTube channel

    2. If you already have a Google account (for Gmail, for example) then you already have a YouTube account. If you don’t want to use your existing Google account for your YouTube channel, you’ll need to create a new Google account.

    You can create a new Google account by:

    1. Going to https://www.youtube.com/
    2. Clicking on your profile picture in the top right corner
    3. Selecting Switch account, then Add account
    4. Selecting Create new account

    Then, you can follow the steps for creating a new Google account. If you have an existing Google account you want to use for your new YouTube channel, you can follow these steps.

    1. Sign into YouTube.
    2. Click on your profile picture in the top right corner.
    3. Click Create a channel.
    4. Choose a Name (this is your display name on YouTube) and a Handle (this is your channel’s URL).
    5. Click Create channel.

      6. It will take a few minutes for your channel to be ready. Once it is, you can view it by clicking Your channel on the left sidebar on YouTube.


  1. Creating a business YouTube channel

    2. Business channels can have more than one manager or owner. You can also connect a channel to your Brand Account if you have one.

    1. Sign into YouTube.
    2. Create a channel by clicking Create a new channel.
    3. To create a channel for a Brand Account, select that Brand Account from the list.
    4. Fill out your channel details.
    5. Click Create.

      6. Read YouTube’s guide to adding or managing more than one owner on a business YouTube channel.
